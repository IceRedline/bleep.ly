# bleep.ly 0.1.2

Faster preview updates after edits, clearer save controls, and interface fixes. For **Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later) running macOS 15.0 Sequoia or later**.

## Preview and Review Words

- Unchecking a word or changing a censor interval updates only the affected audio windows. Source audio is prepared once per session, without repeating recognition or rendering the entire recording after every edit. The prepared preview keeps playing during updates and switches at the current playback position. Undo reuses a cached version or applies a local update.
- Unchecked words remain in **Review Words → Flagged** with a **Censoring off** label, so they can be enabled again. This state is saved in the draft, and the header shows separate counts for detected and selected words.
- Existing mute boundaries, overlapping intervals, and full custom-sound tails are preserved. Each sound plays once without looping or stretching; speech resumes on its original schedule.

## Saving and interface

- Fast Mode offers **Save as Video** and **Save as Audio** for video, and only **Save as Audio** for audio. Automatic saving and the ambiguous Save as action have been removed. Each button opens a destination dialog; both formats can be exported without repeating recognition.
- Advanced Mode places **Export as** beside **Export** on the right, with censor-sound selection on the left. Labels and format controls are aligned, including for audio-only sources.
- The timeline, playback slider, and time labels are aligned. The playhead stays centered over the slider thumb when seeking and resizing the window, including on macOS 15.
- **Custom** immediately activates the previously selected sound. Click the filename to choose a replacement; cancelling the dialog preserves the previous sound and mode.

## Reliability

- Recognition caches are invalidated after updates, rebuilds, and reinstallation of the same build. Normal relaunches retain cache hits. Downloaded models, settings, and saved reviews are preserved.
- Failing or cancelling an attempt to open another file restores playback of the previous review.
- Invalid word and speaker intervals that start beyond the recording are rejected.
- Saving a JSON transcript through the CLI no longer overwrites a file created concurrently.

## Installation

Open `bleep.ly-0.1.2-arm64.dmg` and drag **bleep.ly** to **Applications**, replacing the previous version. The app and DMG are signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Python, FFmpeg, and recognition engines are included; models download separately.

Files: `bleep.ly-0.1.2-arm64.dmg`, `SHA256SUMS`, and the `ffmpeg-8.1.2.tar.xz` source archive (LGPL).
