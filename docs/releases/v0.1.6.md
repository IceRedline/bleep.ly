# bleeply 0.1.6

Faster exports and improved GigaAM word timing on **Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 15.0 or later**.

- **Faster export for long recordings.** Mix censorship audio in one-second blocks instead of creating a full-length audio branch for each detection. Single-track WAV export also skips the full-length intermediate file. A local 77-minute recording with 908 flagged words exported with a custom sound in **3.3 seconds**; performance depends on your Mac and recording.
- **Recover words at speech-chunk boundaries.** Add overlapping context to GigaAM recognition windows and reconcile duplicate boundary words. This recovered a previously missed word in the checked recording.
- **Improve censor timing with Qwen3-ForcedAligner.** Align new GigaAM transcripts to the audio so delayed punctuation does not stretch word timestamps. Retry questionable alignments with nearby context; if alignment remains unreliable, keep the original word and timing for review. The four reported problem spots were checked on short clips and in the full recording. This does not guarantee perfect timing for every word.
- **Refresh recognition results after the timing changes.** Invalidate older GigaAM recognition caches. Existing saved reviews retain their manual edits and mute intervals; reanalyze the original recording to use the new timing algorithm.
- **Preserve audio and editing behavior.** Keep complete custom sounds at their original speed, resume speech at the selected mute endpoint, and preserve overlapping fades, stereo audio, independent video audio tracks, and video stream copying.

GigaAM now also uses the pinned Qwen3-ForcedAligner model, a separate download of approximately **1.84 GB** if it is not already installed. The existing model-download permission applies. Recognition and alignment run locally and work offline once the models are available. The full 77-minute validation took **7 minutes 15 seconds** for recognition and alignment on the test Mac.

## Installation

Open `bleeply-0.1.6-arm64.dmg` and drag the app to **Applications**, replacing the previous version. Quit the previous app before opening the update. The app and DMG are signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Python, FFmpeg, and recognition engines are included; models download separately after permission. Recordings stay on your Mac.

Direct download: version **0.1.6**, build **15**. This release does not publish a new TestFlight build.

Files: `bleeply-0.1.6-arm64.dmg`, `SHA256SUMS`, and `ffmpeg-8.1.2.tar.xz` (LGPL source archive).

This is a historical release. Its signed installer retains the application name used when it was built; the current project name is **bleeply**.
