# bleep.ly 0.1.5

More room for long recordings on **Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 15.0 or later**.

- Increase the temporary preview audio budget from **4 GiB to 7 GiB per session**. This accommodates the storage reservation for a 77-minute recording and an update while its previous preview remains playable.
- Keep automatic cleanup of older preview variants, the eight-variant limit, and the 1 GiB free-disk reserve. Recognition cache and history still share a 256 MiB budget and 30-day retention.
- Store the development GPU Python environment in `.runtime/asr-env`, outside the disposable `.cache` folder. Clearing development caches no longer removes GigaAM dependencies. Existing source checkouts should rerun `scripts/setup-experimental-gpu.sh` to recreate their environment at the new path.
- Downloadable apps continue to include both Python runtimes and recognition engines. Missing model weights download after permission.

Models, original recordings, exports, custom sounds, saved reviews, approved mute boundaries, and complete custom-sound playback are preserved.

## Installation

Open `bleep.ly-0.1.5-arm64.dmg` and drag **bleep.ly** to **Applications**, replacing the previous version. The app and DMG are signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Python, FFmpeg, and recognition engines are included; models download separately after permission. Recordings stay on your Mac.

Direct download: version **0.1.5**, build **14**. This release does not publish a new TestFlight build.

Files: `bleep.ly-0.1.5-arm64.dmg`, `SHA256SUMS`, and `ffmpeg-8.1.2.tar.xz` (LGPL source archive).
