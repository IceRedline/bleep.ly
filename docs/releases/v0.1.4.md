# bleeply 0.1.4

Automatic storage management for **Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 15.0 or later**.

- Temporary preview audio is limited to **4 GiB per session** and eight recent variants. Older disposable audio is removed first, preserving current playback and the inputs needed to update it.
- Recognition cache and history share a **256 MiB** budget and **30-day** retention. Cleanup runs before and after analysis; reusing a transcript refreshes its retention date.
- Abandoned preview and processing folders are cleaned up after a crash. Active app instances and workers keep their temporary files.
- If a preview cannot fit the budget or leave 1 GiB free on disk, the app explains the limit. Original playback and export remain available.
- Settings now explains storage limits. Models, original recordings, exports, custom sounds, and the saved review are preserved. Source videos are never copied into the preview cache.

Includes the packaging, file-permission, and model-download improvements from 0.1.3. Approved mute boundaries and complete custom-sound playback remain unchanged.

## Installation

Open `bleeply-0.1.4-arm64.dmg` and drag the app to **Applications**, replacing the previous version. The app and DMG are signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Python, FFmpeg, and recognition engines are included; models download separately after permission. Recordings stay on your Mac.

Direct download: version **0.1.4**, build **13**. This release does not publish a new TestFlight build.

Files: `bleeply-0.1.4-arm64.dmg`, `SHA256SUMS`, and `ffmpeg-8.1.2.tar.xz` (LGPL source archive).

This is a historical release. Its signed installer retains the application name used when it was built; the current project name is **bleeply**.
