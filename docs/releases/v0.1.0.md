# bleep.ly 0.1.0

The first installable release of **bleep.ly** brings local profanity censoring for video and audio to Mac.

## Installation

1. Download `bleep.ly-0.1.0-arm64.dmg`.
2. Open the disk image and drag **bleep.ly** to **Applications**.
3. Enable **Settings → Allow first-time model download**. The selected model downloads when you process your first file, requiring an internet connection and several GB of free space. Recognition works offline after the download.

**Requirements:** Apple Silicon Mac, **macOS 26.2 or later**. This macOS requirement comes from the bundled MLX library. This release does not include an Intel build.

The app and DMG are signed with Developer ID Application and notarized by Apple. Python, FFmpeg/FFprobe, and recognition engines are included; Homebrew and the project source are not required. Model weights are downloaded separately.

## Features

- **Fast Mode:** drop a file to run recognition, censoring, and export automatically.
- **Advanced:** review words on the timeline, adjust intervals, add missed words, and remove false positives.
- Russian and experimental English support, with editable word lists and exclusions.
- Beep, silence, or a custom sound. The selected sound plays once in full; speech resumes at its original time even if the sound tail is still playing.
- Multiple audio tracks, video export without re-encoding the video stream, and WAV export.
- CPU/Whisper, Apple GPU/MLX, GigaAM, and experimental Parakeet, Qwen, and WhisperKit backends.

Recordings are processed on your Mac. Models, drafts, and caches are stored in `~/Library/Application Support/bleep.ly/`. Automatic recognition can miss words or produce false positives; use Advanced to review the result.

## Release files

- **DMG:** installer for Apple Silicon.
- **SHA256SUMS:** file checksums.
- **ffmpeg-8.1.2.tar.xz:** the exact, unmodified source archive for the bundled FFmpeg (LGPL). Build settings and licenses are available in the bleep.ly source and inside the app.
